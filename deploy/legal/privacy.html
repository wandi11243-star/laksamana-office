Privacy Policy

Laksamana Campaign · Effective 13 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Laksamana Muda ("we", "us") collects and uses information through Laksamana Campaign, an internal dashboard used by our marketing team to review the performance of our own official social media accounts, including our TikTok account. The tool is not offered to the public.

1. Who uses the app

Only authorized Laksamana Muda staff who sign in to our internal office system can access Laksamana Campaign. The only TikTok account connected to the app is Laksamana Muda's own official brand account.

2. Information we collect from TikTok

When an authorized staff member connects our TikTok account through "Login with TikTok", we request only the following permissions:

  • user.info.basic — account open ID, display name, and profile picture.
  • user.info.profile — the account's bio, profile link, and verified status.
  • user.info.stats — the account's follower, following, total likes, and video counts.
  • video.list — the list of public videos posted by the account, including video ID, description/title, cover image, share link, posting time, duration, and the view, like, comment, and share counts.

We also store the access token and refresh token issued by TikTok so the dashboard can refresh these statistics. We do not collect passwords, private messages, follower lists, or any information about people who watch or interact with our videos.

3. How we use the information

  • To show our marketing team which of our own videos perform best.
  • To write internal content insights and plan our posting calendar.
  • To generate content-idea suggestions with an AI service (see section 4).

We do not use this information for advertising to third parties, and we never sell it.

4. Sharing with service providers

To produce content insights, the titles and performance numbers of our own videos, together with our brand profile and research topics, may be sent to our AI service provider, Meta (Muse Spark, Meta Model API). When we use Meta's discounted Contributor tier, Meta may use these inputs and the generated outputs to train and improve its models. Apart from this, information is not shared with anyone outside Laksamana Muda, unless required by law.

5. Storage and security

Information is stored in our own database on our hosting server. Access tokens are kept on the server only and are never shown in the browser. Access to the dashboard requires a staff login.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Disconnecting the TikTok account in the dashboard immediately deletes the stored access and refresh tokens and stops all further data collection.
  • You can also revoke access at any time from the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.
  • Video statistics already collected are kept as internal performance history. They are deleted on request.

7. Contact

For questions or deletion requests, contact Laksamana Muda through our official TikTok or Instagram account @laksamanamuda.id.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
