Privacy Policy

Laksamana Campaign · Effective 18 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Laksamana Muda ("we", "us") collects and uses information through Laksamana Campaign, an internal dashboard used by our marketing team to review the performance of our own official social media accounts, including our TikTok account. The tool is not offered to the public.

1. Who uses the app

Only authorized Laksamana Muda staff who sign in to our internal office system can access Laksamana Campaign. The only TikTok account connected to the app is Laksamana Muda's own official brand account.

2. Information we collect from TikTok

When an authorized staff member connects our TikTok account through "Login with TikTok", we request only the following permissions:

  • user.info.basic — account open ID, display name, and profile picture.
  • user.info.profile — the account's bio, profile link, and verified status.
  • user.info.stats — the account's follower, following, total likes, and video counts.
  • video.list — the list of public videos posted by the account, including video ID, description/title, cover image, share link, posting time, duration, and the view, like, comment, and share counts.

We also store the access token and refresh token issued by TikTok so the dashboard can refresh these statistics. We do not collect passwords, private messages, follower lists, or any information about people who watch or interact with our videos.

Information we collect from Instagram

When an authorized staff member connects our Instagram professional account through Instagram Business Login, we request only these permissions:

  • instagram_business_basic — account ID, username, name, profile picture, bio, follower/following and media counts, and the list of the account's own posts (caption, media type, permalink, thumbnail, posting time, like and comment counts).
  • instagram_business_manage_insights — per-post performance metrics of our own posts (views, reach, saves, and shares) and account-level daily totals (views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves, total interactions, and new followers) of our own account.

We store the long-lived access token issued by Instagram so the dashboard can refresh these statistics. Disconnecting the Instagram account in the dashboard immediately deletes the stored token. You can also remove the app from Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites. To request deletion of collected data, contact us (section 7).

Information we collect from Meta Ads

With a system-user token (ads_read) created in our own Meta Business Manager, the dashboard reads performance reports of our own ad accounts: ad, ad set and campaign names, status, creative thumbnail and linked Instagram post, spend, impressions, reach, clicks, CTR, CPM, frequency, and result actions (e.g. messaging conversations, link clicks, video views). We do not read data about the people who saw or interacted with the ads.

Information we collect from Google Business Profile

When an authorized staff member connects the Google account that manages our own Google Business Profile, we request only one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/business.manage. With it the dashboard reads the list of business locations managed by that account (name, address, place ID, verification state) and the Google reviews written about our own locations: reviewer display name and profile photo as shown publicly on Google, star rating, review text, review time, and our existing reply. The same permission is used to post, edit, or delete our business's reply to a review — only when a staff member presses the reply button. We store the access token and refresh token issued by Google so the dashboard can refresh reviews. We never change business information, and we do not read data about any business other than our own.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. How we use the information

  • To show our marketing team which of our own videos perform best.
  • To write internal content insights and plan our posting calendar.
  • To generate content-idea suggestions with an AI service (see section 4).
  • To let our team read, prioritize, and reply to customer reviews of our own locations from one place.

We do not use this information for advertising to third parties, and we never sell it.

4. Sharing with service providers

To produce content insights, the titles and performance numbers of our own videos, together with our brand profile and research topics, may be sent to our AI service provider, Meta (Muse Spark, Meta Model API). When we use Meta's discounted Contributor tier, Meta may use these inputs and the generated outputs to train and improve its models. When a staff member asks for a review analysis or a reply draft, the review text, star rating, and date (and, for a reply draft, the reviewer's public display name) are also sent to the same provider. Google user data is never used to train generalized AI models on our side, never sold, and never used for advertising. Apart from this, information is not shared with anyone outside Laksamana Muda, unless required by law.

5. Storage and security

Information is stored in our own database on our hosting server. Access tokens are kept on the server only and are never shown in the browser. Access to the dashboard requires a staff login.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Disconnecting the TikTok account in the dashboard immediately deletes the stored access and refresh tokens and stops all further data collection.
  • You can also revoke access at any time from the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.
  • Video statistics already collected are kept as internal performance history. They are deleted on request.
  • Disconnecting Google in the dashboard immediately deletes the stored Google access and refresh tokens. You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Reviews already collected are kept as internal history and are deleted on request.

7. Contact

For questions or deletion requests, contact Laksamana Muda through our official TikTok or Instagram account @laksamanamuda.id.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.

Kebijakan Privasi (Bahasa Indonesia)

Laksamana Campaign adalah dasbor internal milik Laksamana Muda untuk membaca performa akun media sosial resmi kami sendiri, termasuk akun TikTok. Hanya staf berwenang yang login ke sistem kantor kami yang dapat mengaksesnya.

  • Data yang diambil dari TikTok: open ID, nama tampilan, foto profil akun (user.info.basic); bio, tautan profil, dan status terverifikasi (user.info.profile); jumlah follower, following, total likes, dan jumlah video (user.info.stats); serta daftar video publik akun beserta judul, sampul, tautan, waktu tayang, durasi, dan jumlah views, likes, komentar, dan share (video.list). Kami tidak mengambil kata sandi, pesan pribadi, maupun data penonton.
  • Data yang diambil dari Instagram: ID, username, nama, foto profil, bio, jumlah follower/following/postingan, dan daftar postingan akun sendiri beserta likes & komentarnya (instagram_business_basic); serta views, reach, saves, dan share tiap postingan (instagram_business_manage_insights).
  • Data yang diambil dari Google Business Profile: dengan satu izin (business.manage), daftar lokasi bisnis yang dikelola akun kami, serta review Google untuk lokasi kami sendiri — nama & foto publik pengulas, bintang, isi review, waktu, dan balasan kami. Izin yang sama dipakai untuk mengirim, mengubah, atau menghapus balasan kami, hanya saat staf menekan tombolnya. Pemakaiannya mengikuti Google API Services User Data Policy, termasuk ketentuan Limited Use.
  • Kegunaan: membaca konten yang paling berhasil, menyusun insight, merencanakan kalender posting, dan meminta saran ide konten dari layanan AI.
  • Pihak ketiga: judul dan angka performa video kami, profil brand, dan topik riset dapat dikirim ke penyedia AI kami, Meta (Muse Spark), untuk dianalisis. Pada tier Contributor Meta, Meta boleh memakai data tersebut untuk melatih modelnya. Saat staf meminta analisa review atau draf balasan, isi review, bintang, tanggal (dan untuk draf balasan, nama publik pengulas) juga dikirim ke penyedia yang sama. Data tidak dijual dan tidak dibagikan ke pihak lain kecuali diwajibkan hukum.
  • Keamanan: disimpan di database server kami sendiri; token akses tidak pernah ditampilkan di peramban.
  • Penghapusan: memutus akun di dasbor langsung menghapus token dan menghentikan pengambilan data. Izin juga bisa dicabut dari aplikasi TikTok, atau untuk Google di myaccount.google.com/permissions. Riwayat statistik yang sudah tersimpan dihapus atas permintaan.
  • Kontak: akun TikTok / Instagram resmi @laksamanamuda.id.
